Kaveri Health

Patient app and clinician console for a 40-clinic network — appointments, records and tele-consults in one calm surface.

The brief

Kaveri Clinics came to us with a system that worked on paper and nowhere else. The constraint was not ambition — it was tolerance: every interaction had to hold under real load, on real devices, for real people at the end of a long day.

The approach

We calibrated for two weeks before drawing anything: interviews, telemetry, a teardown of the existing stack. Then design and engineering ran as one loop, shipping weekly to a staging build the client team could open on their phones — so every decision was made against the real thing, never a mock-up of it.
